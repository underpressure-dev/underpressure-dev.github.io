Products

More orders. Less effort.

ARGUS works inside the business and operates your ERP. KAIROS works towards customers and brings in new ones. Together they cover the path from the first search to the finished delivery.

01

Two products

A

ARGUS · Inside the business

The digital employee

Sees everything. Forgets nothing.

ARGUS operates your existing ERP like a good employee and runs the business: enquiries, quotes, orders, planning board, machines, staff, stock and quality. With a new, clear interface. Nothing is rebuilt.

  • For Navision, Business Central, SAP, Abacus, Proffix, Sage and bexio
  • As extensions: planning board, machines with OEE, staff, stock and quality
  • Modules can be activated individually, emergency switch and log
  • Workshop tablet in your people's language
More about ARGUSLive demo
K

KAIROS · Towards customers

The customer machine

At the right moment.

KAIROS finds companies that need your services and makes sure you are found on Google and in AI answers. It answers enquiries in minutes and follows up until the order arrives.

  • Radar: new customers from public sources
  • SEO with a page for every service and region
  • Website with instant price and instant reply
  • Follow-up, tenders and customer portal
More about KAIROSLive demo
02

Together

KAIROS wins the order, ARGUS handles it.

An enquiry from the instant price goes straight to ARGUS and becomes a quote, an order and a production order in your ERP. The status flows back to the customer portal, and your customer always sees where the order stands.

Both products also work on their own. Try it: send an enquiry in the KAIROS demo and turn it into a quote in the ARGUS demo.

03

Who it is for

  • SMEs in manufacturing and trades, for example metal, mechanical engineering and apparatus construction
  • Businesses that work with an existing ERP and want to keep it
  • Management that wants more orders without more people in the office
  • Teams that want to retype less and produce more

Behind ARGUS and KAIROS is the same way of working as in all our projects: listen first, then build in stages, and never overturn what works.

04

Introduction in stages

  1. Conversation and site visit

    We look at how quotes, orders, the workshop and customer acquisition work today and where most time is lost.

  2. Technical clarification

    ERP version, licence, interfaces and your calculation. Only then do we set a fixed price per stage, without surprises.

  3. First module live

    We start with the module that works fastest, for example website with instant price, order intake or follow-up.

  4. Test the connection

    We connect ARGUS to a test environment of your ERP and check every step before anything runs in everyday work.

  5. Expansion in stages

    More modules once the first one is settled. You pay per stage and decide again after each one.
