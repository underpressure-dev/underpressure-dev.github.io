Do not leave orders to chance

Many good businesses live on referrals. That works until it stops working.

Buyers today search online, ask an AI and expect a price in minutes rather than days. Anyone who does not show up or only answers after a week is out, even if their work would be better.

KAIROS turns your business into a company that gets found, answers at once and keeps at it. Every day, without your team having to free up time for it.

What KAIROS does every day

  • Searches public sources for new target companies
  • Maintains your visibility on Google and in AI answers
  • Calculates prices on your website in seconds
  • Confirms and answers enquiries around the clock
  • Follows up open quotes
  • Reads new public tenders

A cycle in five steps

  1. Find

    The radar searches the commercial register, building applications, public tenders, company websites and press releases for companies with demand and scores each one.

  2. Get found

    A page for every service and region, technically clean and with structured data. That is how Google and AI assistants find you.

  3. Answer at once

    A price in seconds on the website, confirmation in minutes, answers to standard questions around the clock.

  4. Keep at it

    Open quotes are followed up, dormant customers woken up and tenders summarised.

  5. Retain

    In the customer portal your customers see status and documents and reorder with one click.

05

Frequently asked questions

Isn't this spam?

No. KAIROS does not send mass emails. Every message goes to a company with a concrete reason, is written personally, approved by your team and contains a simple unsubscribe.

Where do the target companies come from?

From public sources such as the commercial register, building applications, public tenders, company websites and press releases. We decide together which sources count for your industry. We only use sources whose terms allow it.

Do we need a new website for this?

Not necessarily. KAIROS can extend your existing website. If it is outdated or slow, we build a new one made for Google, AI assistants and the instant price.

How quickly does SEO work?

Technical improvements often work within weeks. New pages usually need a few months to reach their position. We measure from the start and show you the trend.

Are the prices in the instant price correct?

The instant price calculates with your rates and rules. Anything outside the rules goes to your team as an enquiry. A price becomes binding with your quote.

Do we own the website?

The content and design of your website belong to you. For the software behind it, for example the instant price and the radar, you receive an unlimited right of use. The source code is deposited with a neutral party.

What does KAIROS cost?

That depends on the modules. After a conversation you receive a quote with a fixed price per stage. You only pay for the modules you use.
