Products · ARGUS

The digital employee for your business.

ARGUS operates your existing ERP like a good employee and runs the whole business: from the enquiry through planning board, machines and staff to stock, dispatch and quality. With a new, clear interface. Nothing is rebuilt.

01

Rebuild nothing. Relieve everything.

Your ERP has been running for years. It is paid for, people know it, the data is in it. The problem is not the ERP, but the work around it.

Retyping enquiries, calculating quotes, creating orders, writing work plans, reordering material, asking where an order stands. This is exactly the work ARGUS takes over. It operates your ERP the way a good employee would and gives your team a clear interface that works on screen and on a tablet.

Whatever needs a decision, ARGUS puts in front of your team. Whatever is routine, it does itself, and every action is logged.

At a glance

  • Works with your existing ERP
  • One place for orders, planning board, machines, staff, stock and quality
  • New interface for office, workshop and management
  • Modules can be activated individually
  • Your team approves, ARGUS does the work
  • Emergency switch: pause it and everything runs as before
  • Runs in your business, the data stays in house
02

From the first email to the machine

  1. Read

    ARGUS reads enquiries and orders from email and PDF, recognises customer, part, material and quantity and checks feasibility.

  2. Calculate

    The calculation follows your rates. Your team checks and approves, only then does the quote go out.

  3. Enter

    Quote, order and production order are in the ERP without anyone retyping a figure. The documents are linked.

  4. Plan

    Work plan, times, material and job card. If material is missing, a purchase proposal is ready.

  5. Execute and measure

    The job appears on the workshop tablet. Times flow back, the cockpit shows utilisation and post-calculation.

03

Twelve modules, one business

Six modules form the core and run from the start. Six more build on them. We check beforehand whether they make sense in your business. Each module can be switched on individually, whatever is switched off, your team does in the ERP as before.

Core: from the start

01

Order intake

Reads enquiries and orders from email and PDF and enters them in the ERP. Nothing gets lost in the inbox any more.

02

Quote and calculation

Calculates with your rates and experience, creates the quote and reminds you to follow up. Your team approves.

03

Job preparation

Work plan, times, material, job card and handover to machines and CAM.

04

Purchasing and material

Reserves material, detects demand, proposes orders and monitors delivery dates.

05

Workshop tablet

Job, drawing and protective equipment in your people's language, with pictures instead of lots of text. Times per job, target and actual.

06

Management cockpit

Utilisation, hit rate, delays and post-calculation per order. You see which orders make money.

Extensions: checked per business and quoted separately

07

Planning board

Detailed scheduling across machines and days, by mouse or automatically. ARGUS checks sequence, staff, material and delivery dates and resolves conflicts.

08

Machines and OEE

Every machine live: running, setting up, idle or faulty. OEE, downtime reasons, part counters, energy and maintenance.

09

Staff and assignment

Attendance, assignment to machines, skills, shift plan, holidays and safety instructions. ARGUS warns before a machine is left unstaffed.

10

Stock and dispatch

Stock with coverage and purchase proposals, remnants matched to orders, goods receipt with certificate, dispatch with invoice in the ERP.

11

Quality

First article inspection with measurements, 8D complaints, gauges and traceability down to the batch.

12

Assistant

Questions in plain language to your company's data: which orders are late? Which machine is down?

04

How ARGUS is built

ARGUS runs in your business and connects what is separate today: ERP, machines, workshop and office.

Connecting machines, older ones too

01

OPC UA

Newer machines provide state, program, counters and messages via the standard interface OPC UA. Whether and how your machine supports it, we check with the manufacturer.

02

Retrofit module

Older machines get a small retrofit module, for example a contactless current sensor. ARGUS then sees whether the machine is running or idle, without touching the control. Electrical work is done by a qualified electrician.

03

Tablet

Manual workstations such as welding or assembly report start, end and downtime reasons on the tablet, with one tap.

05

Two routes to the ERP

From large to small: Navision and Business Central, SAP, Abacus, Proffix, Sage and bexio.

01

Official interface

Web services, REST or OData, as offered by your ERP. The cleanest and most stable route. We always use it when it exists.

02

Read and import module

With older versions ARGUS reads via the database or an export and writes via a small import module that your ERP partner installs. ARGUS never writes directly into the database.

08

Frequently asked questions

Do we have to replace our ERP?

No. ARGUS works with your existing ERP. Your data, your documents and your processes in the ERP stay.

Which ERP systems does ARGUS support?

Microsoft Dynamics NAV (Navision) and Business Central, SAP, Abacus, Proffix, Sage and bexio. Which route is right for you, we check with your version before the project.

What happens if ARGUS makes a mistake?

Anything that goes out is approved by your team. Every action is logged and traceable. With the emergency switch you can carry on working directly in the ERP at any time.

Where does our data run?

ARGUS runs in your business. For AI features you choose whether a local model or a service is used and which data is released for it.

What is the difference to a new ERP?

A new ERP means data migration, training and often a long changeover. ARGUS builds on what you have and brings value from the first module.

Do we also need an MES?

Not necessarily. Planning board, machine data, staff and quality are ARGUS extension modules. If you already have an MES or time recording, we first check whether ARGUS can connect to it instead of replacing it.

Can old machines be connected too?

Usually yes. Newer machines often provide their data via OPC UA. Older machines get a retrofit module, for example a contactless current sensor, without touching the control. Manual workstations report via the tablet. We check beforehand what works with your machines.

May we record staff like this?

Working time, assignments and skills may be recorded. Systems that monitor employee behaviour are not allowed in Switzerland, and ARGUS is built not to do that. We clarify the details for your business with you.

How far along is ARGUS?

We show the interface and processes in the live demo. We set up the connection to your ERP for your business and test it in a test environment before ARGUS works in everyday operation.

Do we own the software?

You receive an unlimited right of use for your business. Your data and your calculation rules belong to you. The software stays with Under Pressure because it is developed further. The source code is deposited with a neutral party, so you are protected.

What does ARGUS cost?

That depends on the modules and your ERP. After the technical clarification you receive a quote with a fixed price per stage. You only pay for the modules you use.
