Software

Software that works for your business.

I have been building websites and applications since 2015, for my own companies and for third parties. I program myself, directly for you, with a manual and no follow-up costs.

01

What I build

Most businesses do not need a huge platform. They need a tool that maps exactly their process, that everyone understands right away and that still runs in five years.

That is exactly what I build. I listen first: how do you work today, where is time lost, where do errors happen? Then I design the simplest solution that really solves the problem. That can be a fast website, a client portal, a time tracking tool or a small controller that monitors a machine.

You work directly with me. I program myself, use AI as a tool and document everything so that you do not depend on me.

Five areas, one way of working

Choose an area. For each one you see typical tasks, how I go about it and what you end up with.

Websites

Your website is your first impression and works around the clock. I build it to load fast, work on every device and be legally sound.

Typical tasks

  • Company site, business card or product page
  • Multilingual, for example German and English
  • Imprint and privacy policy under Swiss law
  • Search engine basics and structured data

How I build it

I write the pages by hand, without a heavy site builder. Fonts and images live on your server, not at Google. If you do not want tracking, you do not need a cookie banner either. I check every page on a phone and a desktop, in light and dark mode.

What you get

A fast, accessible website that you can maintain yourself or that I adapt for you. I also set up the domain, email forwarding and search engine listing.

  • HTML
  • CSS
  • JavaScript
  • GitHub Pages
  • Schema.org
  • DNS

Web apps and portals

When clients exchange files, staff sign in or data needs to be managed cleanly, you need an application rather than a website.

Typical tasks

  • Client portal with file exchange and sharing links
  • Sign-in, roles and access rights
  • Dashboards with key figures
  • Interfaces to existing systems

How I build it

The application runs in the browser, without installation. Sign-in goes through an upstream access service, and the server checks permissions on every request. Every important action is logged and data is backed up daily.

What you get

A tool your clients and your team understand right away, and a manual that describes how it is built and how to run it.

  • Cloudflare Workers
  • Cloudflare Access
  • Backblaze B2
  • Next.js
  • PostgreSQL

Business software

Time tracking, document control, expenses, holiday planning: off-the-shelf software costs licences and still never quite fits. I build the solution for your process.

Typical tasks

  • Time tracking with flextime, holidays and expenses
  • Document control with approval workflow and versions
  • Online manuals and audit reports
  • Exports in the format you already use today

How I build it

I start with your current process, for example the Excel list everyone knows. It becomes an application with a database that calculates, checks and reminds. In the end the export looks like what your bookkeeping already knows.

What you get

Less paperwork, fewer errors and figures that add up. The live demos on this site show what it looks like in practice.

  • Next.js
  • Prisma
  • PostgreSQL
  • Vercel
  • Excel-Export

03

How a solution is structured

A typical application consists of a few clearly separated parts. The browser shows the interface. Access protection checks who you are. The application contains the rules of your business, calculates and writes a log. Data lives in a database, files in their own storage, and both are backed up every day.

This separation makes the solution robust and understandable. Each part can be checked, replaced or extended on its own. That is exactly how it is described in the manual.
