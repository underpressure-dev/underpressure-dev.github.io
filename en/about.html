About me

I am Mike.

A developer working from home. And someone who climbs a mountain at 4 a.m. to fly back down at 150 km/h. Both belong together.

06

Pico ballooning

As a counterweight I do pico ballooning. Right now I launch weather probes on high-altitude balloons and collect data.

The next goal is big: a probe to the North Pole. Delivery works with two pico balloons. If the wind carries the probe far enough north, one balloon is cut away. The probe then does not even need a parachute. The data reaches me by radio, via the ionosphere.

Tracker and delivery are in testing. This will be a long chapter of my story, not a short hobby.
