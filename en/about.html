Company

Under Pressure from Lucerne.

Software and web, FPV aerial footage and diving work. With one fixed contact person from the first question to operation.

01

Profile

Under Pressure was founded in Lucerne in 2025. At its core is software that solves a concrete task in a business: websites, portals, business software and automation.

Add to that FPV aerial footage with drones of our own design, and industrial and emergency diving, which has its own website at industrietaucher.ch.

For our clients this means: whoever talks to you also does the work. Every solution is documented so that you do not depend on us.

Why Under Pressure? The name comes from diving, where work is done under pressure. It stands for what we aim for: working calmly and precisely, even when things get tight.

Management

Michael Küng founded Under Pressure and runs the company.

The background combines design, electronics and software: from the apprenticeship as a design engineer through eight years as FPV operator, test pilot and developer for an international drone team to the executive board of DTD Systems GmbH.

Add to that talks on drone technology, including at the Verkehrshaus Luzern, the diploma as a blasting specialist and training as a technical diver.
