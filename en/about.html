About me

I am Mike.

Entrepreneur and problem solver from Lucerne. Software, drones, diving work and blasting. In my free time I am mostly in the mountains.

01

Profile

I am both: wild boar on the mountain, man in a suit at the desk.

I am an entrepreneur and I solve problems: with software, with drones, under water or with blasting. For you that means one contact person who listens and does the work himself.

I trained as a design engineer. For eight years I flew, tested and helped develop drones for an international team, and I give talks.

Why Under Pressure? Because I perform especially well under pressure, just like when diving.

02

Speedflying

Speedflying is my passion.

At 4 a.m. I head into the mountains, for example to the Jungfrau or the Mönch, and fly back down at around 150 km/h. I have been at it for two years, in the air four to five times a week.

Paragliding is part of who I am anyway. Add ski touring and mountaineering, with avalanche, ski touring and mountaineering courses.
