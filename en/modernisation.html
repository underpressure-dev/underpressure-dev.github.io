Business modernisation

A fresh wind for established businesses.

Many companies have worked well for decades, but with yesterday's tools. We bring modern software, AI and new customer acquisition into your business, without overturning what works.

01

Why now

The work is good, the customers are loyal. But the world outside changes faster than the processes inside.

Anyone looking for a supplier today searches online and expects a price quickly. Anyone looking for skilled staff competes with modern employers. This is exactly where we start: with software that takes over routine work, and with customer acquisition that no longer depends on chance alone.

Typical starting point

  • Quotes and job preparation cost many hours every week.
  • New customers come almost only through referrals.
  • The website is outdated and hardly found.
  • Data is scattered across the ERP, Excel lists and a few people's heads.
  • Skilled staff are hard to find, and the future of the business needs securing.
02

What we build

A complete package made of building blocks. You choose what your business needs and start where it brings the most.

01

Shop window

A new website with real photos, services, references and jobs, found on Google.

02

Customer acquisition

Search engines, Google Business Profile, targeted ads, target customer lists, public tenders and automatic follow-up of open quotes.

03

Instant price and quote

Customers get a price in seconds. Quotes are created automatically from your rates, your team approves them.

04

Automatic job preparation

Work plans, materials and job documents are created automatically and go straight to machines and the ERP.

05

Customer portal

Your customers see order status, drawings and documents and reorder with one click.

06

Management cockpit

Post-calculation per order, utilisation, hit rate and a monthly report. You see which orders make money.

07

Workshop tablet

Job, tools and protective equipment in your people's language, with pictures instead of lots of text. Plus hours, target and actual per job.

08

AI in everyday work

Assistants that sort enquiries, evaluate documents and translate. Your data stays under your control.

09

One contact

From the first idea to operation: whoever plans with you also builds the solution.

03

How we work

01

We rebuild nothing

Your ERP, your machines and your proven processes stay. The new software connects to them and takes over the routine.

02

An emergency switch

If something does not work, the software is pushed aside and your team carries on as before.

03

In stages

Every stage delivers value on its own. You pay per stage and decide again after each one.

04

Calculated first

We calculate the benefit with your own figures before you invest.

05

Data in house

Where it makes sense, the software runs on a computer in your business, without an external server.

06

Your team is on board

Training, a manual and operation in several languages. The new software should relieve people, not burden them.

04

Process

  1. Conversation and site visit

    We listen and look at how your business works today: quotes, orders, workshop, customers.

  2. Your own demo

    You see your company before you invest: a live demo with your photos, your services and your processes, password protected.

  3. Business case

    With your figures: what it brings, what it costs and when it pays back.

  4. Implementation in stages

    First whatever works fastest, usually the website and customer acquisition.

  5. Support

    After the rollout we remain your contact, for questions, adjustments and new ideas.
