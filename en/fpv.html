FPV and drones

Where other cameras stop, I start.

Since 2016 I have been flying and building racing drones with my own components and software. The hobby turned into film productions, record flights and talks.

01

From hobby to record flight

At some point I thought: this exists, but it can be done much better.

So we pushed it to the limit, with our own components and our own software. I was one of the first to offer footage shot with racing drones. Large companies and car brands have used my services.

My best drones are on permanent display in Swiss museums today. I also give talks there.

03

Milestones

  1. May 2019

    First FPV alpine crossing

    First documented FPV drone world record flight across the Swiss Alps.

  2. Aug. 2020

    Matterhorn world record project

    FPV operation above 4000 m a.s.l., planned under extreme weather and altitude conditions.

  3. 2021

    Eiger north face

    First documented FPV drone flight over the Eiger north face.

  4. Since 2019

    Further records

    Distance and altitude records with FPV and autonomous drones, including a night flight over alpine terrain with thermal and image intensifier systems.

04

What I offer

Footage

Moving images no other camera can capture: vehicles, buildings, landscapes and events up close and at high speed.

Custom builds

Drones from custom components, with firmware, telemetry and sensors for special applications.

Talks

Talks on drone technology and FPV systems for expert and general audiences, for example at the Verkehrshaus Luzern.
