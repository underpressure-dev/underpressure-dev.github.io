FPV and drones

FPV and racing drones

For over 10 years I have been building racing drones with my own components and software, and filming with them.

01

What I do

At some point I thought: this exists, but it can be done much better. So we pushed it to the limit, with our own components and our own software.

I was one of the first to offer footage shot with racing drones. Large companies and car brands have used my services.

My best drones are on permanent display in Swiss museums today. I also give talks there.

  • 10+years of racing drones
  • 5world records
02

Milestones

  • May 2019First FPV alpine crossing. First documented FPV drone world record flight across the Swiss Alps.
  • Aug. 2020Matterhorn world record project. FPV operation above 4000 m a.s.l.
  • 2021First FPV drone flight over the Eiger north face.
  • 2019 to dateFurther documented distance and altitude records with FPV and autonomous drones in Switzerland, including a night flight record over alpine terrain with thermal and image intensifier systems.

03

What I offer

Footage

Moving images no other camera can capture: vehicles, buildings, landscapes and events from the drone's perspective.

Custom builds

Drones from custom components, with custom firmware and software.

Talks

How racing drones work and how to push them to the limit.
