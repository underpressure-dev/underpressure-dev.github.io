AlfaOS · Autopilot for submarines

The autopilot for under water.

AlfaOS is firmware and a configurator for remote-controlled submarines. It stabilises, holds depth and runs missions on its own, even where no radio can reach. Everything is configured in the browser, with no installation.

The principle

100 HzControl loop with watchdog
5Operating modes, failsafe included
64Steps per mission
58Parameters, all adjustable

Built like iNav. Made for under water.

Anyone who flies planes or multirotors with iNav knows the principle: firmware in the vehicle, a configurator on the PC, clear modes, a mission planner and a failsafe that takes over in an emergency.

AlfaOS carries this principle under water, where more comes into play: buoyancy and ballast, pressure instead of altitude, and no radio below the surface. The boat has to be able to decide on its own.

We know iNav from our own practice: our long-range PC-7 flies with it.

02

Five modes, one clear sequence

From driving by hand to the autonomous mission. And above it all the failsafe, which always takes priority.

01

Manual

Direct control from the transmitter. Rudders and thrust follow the pilot.

02

Stabilised

A PID controller holds the pitch angle, with the bow planes mixed in.

03

Depth

AlfaOS holds the set depth with dive planes, sink-rate damping and ballast. When the boat stops, the pump holds the depth.

04

Mission

The boat runs a planned mission step by step, autonomously and, if you choose, entirely without radio.

05

Failsafe

On a leak, critical battery, depth limit, sensor failure or loss of radio, AlfaOS takes over: empty the ballast, nose up 10°, 15 % throttle, surface.

03

Missions

Up to 64 steps

Radio does not reach under water. So the mission is planned beforehand, sent to the boat, and AlfaOS runs it autonomously. Building blocks:

  • Wait
  • Dive
  • Straight
  • Turn
  • Curve with radius
  • Ballast
  • Fixed rudder
  • Surface
  • Repeat

Know what happens before the start

  • 3D preview of the track, with playback
  • Depth profile and top view
  • Duration, distance, maximum depth and energy demand before the start
  • The planner limits throttle and pitch like the firmware and warns when a depth exceeds the limit
  • Send, store in the boat, read back
04

The configurator

One tool for everything, right in the browser and with no installation.

The configurator reads the parameter list directly from the firmware source code when it is built. So boat and configurator always speak the same language.

Open configuratorDemo without a boat
  • Overview with attitude, heading, depth and all outputs
  • Live graphs for attitude, heading, depth, battery, leak and ballast
  • Calibration: depth, attitude, IMU profile, battery voltage, servo centre, end points and direction
  • Tests for servos, motor and pump, only when disarmed and with a watchdog
  • 58 parameters with minimum, maximum, unit and description, with import and export
  • Stored in flash with a checksum, telemetry from 0 to 50 Hz
  • Terminal for every protocol command
  • Demo boat: try everything, with no hardware at all
05

Safety built in

Failsafe takes priority

Leaks, battery, depth limit, sensor failure and radio loss are monitored continuously. One trigger is enough, and the boat surfaces.

Leak probe

Two measuring points at the lowest point of the pressure hull report incoming water, and the failsafe steps in.

Safe test mode

Servos, motor and pump can only be driven directly when disarmed. If the signal stops for 1.5 s, the firmware stops everything.

Limits in software

Rudder deflection, pitch, throttle and depth are limited, identically in the firmware and in the mission planner.

Recovery aid

A strobe sits in a separate pod with its own battery and reed switch, independent of the boat.

Watchdog

The control loop runs at 100 Hz and is supervised by a watchdog.

Hardware

The boat Alfa, on which AlfaOS is being developed.

Electronics

Computer
RP2040 Zero, 100 Hz, watchdog
Attitude
BNO055, IMU mode without compass, NDOF optional
Depth and temperature
MS5837-30BA, second-order compensation
Radio
Crossfire (CRSF, 420000 baud), 8 channels, RSSI
Connection
USB for configuration, diagnostics and missions

Propulsion and ballast

Motor
AS2304 1800 KV, wet-running
ESC
3D mode, forward and reverse, adjustable dead band
Rudders
3 × MG90S: rudder, stern planes, bow planes
Ballast
Peristaltic pump, fills and empties via an H-bridge
Power
2S 850 mAh, XT60, voltage sensing

Construction

Hull
SLA resin, printed
Pressure tube
50 mm inside, 400 mm long

Safety

separate pod with its own battery

In development

Status

AlfaOS 0.1 is a draft. The firmware core and configurator are tested on a PC, with test models instead of hardware: protocol, arming, failsafe, test mode and a complete mission against a boat model. Tests in the water are still to come. The controller values are starting values and will be tuned in the water.
