AlfaOS · Firmware and configurator

An operating system for a 1:120 scale submarine.

AlfaOS runs our remote-controlled submarine Alfa. The firmware runs in the boat, the configurator directly in the browser. It needs no server and no installation.

Open configuratorDemo without a boat
01

What AlfaOS does

Firmware

Runs on an RP2040 Zero. It controls depth, pitch and heading, drives rudders, motor and ballast pump, and runs missions on its own.

Configurator

Connects via USB straight from the browser. Sensors, calibration, tests, parameters and mission planning in one interface, with no installation.

Missions

Radio does not reach under water. Deep missions are planned in the configurator, sent to the boat and run autonomously. The planner limits throttle and pitch the same way the firmware does.

Safety

Test mode only when disarmed, motor and pump with a watchdog. On a leak, flat battery, depth limit, sensor failure or loss of radio: empty the ballast, nose up, surface.

02

How to start

  • Chrome or Edge on a PC or Mac, for the USB connection to the boat
  • Firefox, Safari and mobile browsers do not support the USB connection (Web Serial)
  • The demo needs no boat: it simulates telemetry, tests and missions

Status

Version 0.1. The firmware core is tested on a PC against test models: protocol, arming, failsafe, test mode and a complete mission against a boat model. AlfaOS has not yet been tested on real hardware or in the water. The controller values are starting values and will only be tuned in the water.
