Web and software

Websites, web apps and custom software

I have been building websites for my own companies and for third parties since 2015. I code from my home office. You do not get an agency, but someone who understands your task, builds it and documents it. Everything on request, no follow-up costs.

01

Websites

Your website is your business card, often the first impression. I build it to load fast, look as good on a phone as on a desktop and be legally sound. On request without cookies, tracking or third-party services, so you do not need a cookie banner.

  • Company site or business card, from one page to many
  • Multilingual, for example German and English with a switch
  • Responsive from 320 pixels wide, with print view
  • Accessibility to WCAG 2.1 level AA as the goal
  • Imprint and privacy policy under Swiss law
  • Search engine basics: clean URLs, sitemap, structured data
  • Fonts and images hosted locally, no calls to Google or others
02

Web apps and portals

When your clients need to exchange files, your team has to sign in or data needs to be managed cleanly, a website is not enough. I build applications with login, roles and permissions that run in the browser, without installation.

  • Client portals with file exchange and sharing links
  • Sign-in, roles and access rights
  • Dashboards and reports
  • Interfaces to existing systems
  • Data backups and traceable logs
03

Business software

Time tracking, document control, expenses, holiday planning: off-the-shelf software costs licences and still never quite fits. I build the solution for your process. The demos on this site show what that looks like.

  • Time tracking with flextime, holidays and expenses
  • Document control with approval workflow and versions
  • Online manuals and audit reports
  • Export to Excel and print views
  • Your data belongs to you and stays with you
04

Hardware and controls

Sometimes software should not just display numbers but move something. I program microcontrollers such as Arduino and Raspberry Pi Pico and controls around CNC. My pico balloons are an example: electronics, radio and software that have to work together.

  • Arduino and Raspberry Pi Pico
  • CNC control and integration
  • Sensors, radio and data logging
  • Custom electronics, including firmware
05

AI applications

I use AI every day as a tool when coding and build it in where it helps you: capturing text, sorting data, answering questions about your documents. No buzzword show, just clear benefit.

  • Assistants for your own documents and processes
  • Automation of recurring tasks
  • Integration with existing software
  • A clear statement of what AI can and cannot do
