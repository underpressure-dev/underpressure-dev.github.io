Manual: Drawing to quote

Purpose

The tool reads a technical drawing and does the groundwork of a quote: material, dimensions, tolerances, processes, open questions and a rough ballpark. This is how the customer machine KAIROS works in practice.

Usage

Drag a drawing into the field or choose a file (PNG, JPG, WebP or PDF). The file is sent to the server only for analysis and is not stored there.

  • Material, dimensions and detected processes
  • Tolerances, surface finish and features such as threads or fits
  • Questions a calculator would ask
  • Assumptions and a rough ballpark per piece

Important

The ballpark is an example, not a quote. It is based on what the AI reads from the drawing and on simple assumptions. A binding quote follows, as always, after review by a person.

Technology

The drawing is read by a language model from Anthropic (Claude). The call runs through a small Under Pressure server, not in the browser. No files and no results are stored.
