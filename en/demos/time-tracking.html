Manual: Time tracking

Purpose

Time tracking records working hours, calculates the flextime balance, manages holidays and absences and collects expenses. Management approves, the report goes to Excel.

Usage

At the top you choose the view: employees see only their own data, management sees everyone and approves requests.

  • Time: record entries with from, to, break and project. The month sheet shows actual, target and difference per day.
  • Holidays: request a period, management approves or rejects.
  • Expenses: record amount, category and note, management approves.
  • Export: monthly report as a CSV file that Excel opens directly.

Calculation

In the demo the target is 8.4 hours per working day (Monday to Friday). Approved holidays and absences count as target met. The flextime balance is the sum of actual minus target since the first entry.

Typical set-up of a real solution

Web application with database, sign-in and roles, run at a provider of your choice. The export layout follows your existing Excel format. In a real solution this manual also describes backup, restore and customisation.
