    About pentesting

    Manual: Security check

    Purpose

    The security check examines a website from outside, the way any browser sees it, and gives a grade from A to F. Each finding comes with a reason and instructions to fix it.

    What is checked

    Only public information: the home page, the redirect to HTTPS, public DNS records and the security.txt file. No attacks are carried out.

    • Encryption: HTTPS, redirect and HSTS.
    • Protection in the browser: Content Security Policy, protection against embedding, other security headers and cookies.
    • Email protection: SPF, DMARC and DKIM against forged senders.
    • Privacy and third parties: known tracking services and link to the privacy policy.
    • Name service: DNSSEC, CAA and IPv6.
    • Disclosed information: version details and security.txt.

    Note on the demo

    The check is real and runs on a check service at Cloudflare. When you open the page you see the report for our own website as an example. Each sender can run 20 checks per hour.

    Limits

    The check does not replace a pentest. Application logic, access rights and vulnerabilities in the software are only examined by a pentest with your written permission.
