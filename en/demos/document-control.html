Manual: Document control

Purpose

Document control makes sure that only reviewed and approved documents apply. It keeps versions, shows approval status and produces an online manual and an audit report from them.

Usage

With "View as" you pick a person with a role (author, reviewer, approver). The client keeps documents of different customers apart.

  • Draft: the author edits the text and submits it for review.
  • Review: the reviewer passes it on for approval or rejects it with a comment.
  • Approval: the approver puts the version into force. The previous version is then marked as superseded.
  • Manual: all approved documents as a readable manual with table of contents, printable.
  • Audit report: status of all documents including overdue reviews.

Typical set-up of a real solution

Web application with database and user management. Every version is stored traceably. The manual of a real solution describes roles, review periods and backup.
