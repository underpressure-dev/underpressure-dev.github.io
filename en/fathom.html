FATHOM The autopilot for under water.

Firmware and configurator for remote-controlled submarines. FATHOM stabilises, holds depth and runs missions on its own, even where no radio can reach. At the surface it finds its own way back with GPS. It is configured over USB or directly through the boat's own WLAN.

01

The principle

Built like iNav. Made for under water.

Anyone who flies planes or multirotors with iNav knows the principle: firmware in the vehicle, a configurator on the PC, clear modes, a mission planner and a failsafe that takes over in an emergency.

FATHOM carries this principle under water, where more comes into play: buoyancy and ballast, pressure instead of altitude, and no radio below the surface. The boat has to be able to decide on its own.

The displays are modelled on a submarine control station: trim and heel indicator, gyrocompass with ordered course and depth gauge with deviation from the set depth.

We know iNav from our own practice: our long-range PC-7 flies with it.

02

Six modes, one clear sequence

From driving by hand to the autonomous mission. And above it all the failsafe, which always takes priority.

01

Manual

Direct control from the transmitter. Rudders and thrust follow the pilot.

02

Stabilised

A PID controller holds the pitch angle, with the bow planes mixed in.

03

Depth

FATHOM holds the set depth with dive planes, sink-rate damping and ballast. When the boat stops, the pump holds the depth.

04

Mission

The boat runs a planned mission step by step, autonomously and, if you choose, entirely without radio.

05

Return to home (RTH)

With GPS, the boat returns to its starting point at the surface. Under water it never navigates by GPS: it surfaces first, waits for a valid fix and then heads home.

06

Failsafe

On a leak, critical battery, depth limit, sensor failure or loss of radio, FATHOM takes over: empty the ballast, nose up 10°, 15 % throttle, surface. On radio loss, optionally with return to home.

03

Missions

Up to 64 steps

Radio does not reach under water. So the mission is planned beforehand, sent to the boat, and FATHOM runs it autonomously. Building blocks:

  • Wait
  • Dive
  • Straight
  • Turn
  • Curve with radius
  • Ballast
  • Fixed rudder
  • Surface
  • Repeat

Know what happens before the start

  • 3D preview of the track, with playback
  • Depth profile and top view
  • Duration, distance, maximum depth and energy demand before the start
  • The planner limits throttle and pitch like the firmware and warns when a depth exceeds the limit
  • Send, store in the boat, read back, over USB or WLAN
04

New in version 0.4

Navigation and black box

Back to the starting point

GPS only works at the surface. So FATHOM only uses it there: the boat surfaces, waits for a valid fix and then returns to the starting point.

  • Home is set on arming, otherwise at the first GPS fix at the surface, or by hand
  • Return to home in phases: surface, wait for GPS, travel, arrived
  • If you choose, the failsafe starts the return to home on radio loss
  • Map without internet: north up, grid in metres, distance and bearing to home

Black box: know afterwards what happened

Under water you cannot see the boat. So the black box records every dive and shows afterwards, step by step, what the boat measured and controlled.

  • Records while armed, 1 to 20 times per second, in the boat's internal memory
  • Read out only when disarmed, over USB or WLAN, with a checksum
  • Charts for depth, attitude, heading, thrust, ballast and rudders, with zoom
  • GPS track, duration, maximum depth, lowest battery voltage and failsafe events
  • Export as CSV, save the raw data as a file
05

WLAN on board

Configure it on the shore, with a phone. No cable, no app, no internet.

A small radio module sits in the boat, a XIAO ESP32 C3. It opens its own WLAN and serves the configurator itself. Phone, tablet or laptop connect straight to the boat, and the pressure hull stays closed.

The radio module talks to the computer over its own port, separate from USB. If the connection drops, the configurator reconnects by itself once the boat is disarmed.

How to connect

  1. Switch the boat on. It must be disarmed.
  2. Join the WLAN FATHOM-XXXX.
  3. Open http://192.168.4.1 in the browser.
  4. Press «Per WLAN verbinden» (connect via WLAN).

This does not work from underpressure.ch: the site is encrypted, and the browser does not allow a connection from there into the boat’s unencrypted network. That is why the boat carries the configurator itself.

Over WLAN

  • Read and write parameters
  • Send missions and store them in the boat
  • Read out the black box
  • Tests and calibration
  • Live telemetry at 10 Hz

Only over Crossfire or USB

  • Arming
  • Mission start
  • Bootloader and firmware update
06

The configurator

One tool for everything, right in the browser and with no installation.

The configurator reads the parameter list directly from the firmware source code when it is built. So boat and configurator always speak the same language, over USB just as over WLAN.

Open configuratorDemo without a boat
  • Overview with instruments modelled on a submarine control station: trim and heel, gyrocompass with ordered course, depth gauge with deviation from the set depth
  • Live graphs for attitude, heading, depth, battery, leak and ballast
  • Calibration: depth, attitude, IMU profile, battery voltage, servo centre, end points and direction
  • Tests for servos, motor and pump, only when disarmed and with a watchdog
  • Navigation: map without internet, set home, start and end return to home
  • Black box: load a recording, charts, GPS track and CSV export
  • 68 parameters with minimum, maximum, unit and description, with import and export
  • Stored in flash with a checksum
  • Terminal for every protocol command
  • Demo boat: try everything, with no hardware at all
07

Safety built in

Failsafe takes priority

Leaks, battery, depth limit, sensor failure and radio loss are monitored continuously. One trigger is enough, and the boat surfaces.

WLAN only when disarmed

When the boat is armed, the firmware switches the WLAN off and keeps it off, even against accidental switching on. Arming and mission start never work over WLAN.

Leak probe

Two measuring points at the lowest point of the pressure hull report incoming water, and the failsafe steps in.

Safe test mode

Servos, motor and pump can only be driven directly when disarmed. If the signal stops for 1.5 s, the firmware stops motor and pump.

Limits in software

Rudder deflection, pitch, throttle and depth are limited, identically in the firmware and in the mission planner.

Recovery aid

A strobe sits in a separate pod with its own battery and reed switch, independent of the boat.

Hardware

The test boat on which FATHOM is being developed.

Electronics

Computer
RP2040 Zero, 100 Hz control loop
Attitude
BNO055, IMU mode without compass, NDOF optional
Depth and temperature
MS5837-30BA, second-order compensation
Remote control
Crossfire (CRSF, 420000 baud), 8 channels, RSSI
GPS
ATGM336H, NMEA at 9600 baud
WLAN
XIAO ESP32 C3, its own network, linked to the Zero by UART at 460800 baud
Connection
USB for configuration, diagnostics and firmware

Propulsion and ballast

Motor
AS2304 1800 KV, wet-running
ESC
forward only by default, reverse by parameter if the ESC supports it
Rudders
3 × MG90S: rudder, stern planes, bow planes
Ballast
Peristaltic pump, fills and empties
Pump
DFRobot DFR0523 with built-in driver, 50 Hz servo signal
Power
2S 850 mAh, XT60, voltage sensing
Supply
logic from the ESC's BEC, servos and pump from a separate 5 V converter

Construction

Hull
SLA resin, printed
Pressure tube
PVC PN16
Sled
Chassis v1.2, battery at the bottom, 4 slide rails
Radio module
its own place on the deck between IMU and Crossfire receiver, antenna flat against the tube wall
Safety
Leak probe, status LED, main switch for now an XT60 plug
Strobe
separate pod with its own battery

  • USB: Chrome or Edge on a PC or Mac (Web Serial). Firefox, Safari and mobile browsers cannot do USB.
  • WLAN: any browser, including on a phone. Join the boat's WLAN and open http://192.168.4.1.
  • The demo needs no boat: it simulates telemetry, tests and missions.

In development

Status

FATHOM 0.4 is a draft. It is tested on a PC, with test models instead of hardware: the firmware core with protocol, arming, failsafe, test mode and a complete mission against a boat model, plus GPS parsing, return to home and the black box format, the locks for the radio port, and the configurator over a simulated WLAN bridge.

Not yet tested: the firmware on real hardware, including GPS and black box, the radio module's program (written, not yet compiled), the WLAN in the real boat and the tests in the water. The controller values are starting values and will be tuned in the water.
